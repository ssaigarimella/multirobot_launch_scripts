2. What the Nov 13 flight showed

Vehicles 1 and 3, 5 min 42 s both in Offboard, outdoor grass, 35 x 35 m fence, 4.0 m apart on the launch line. Full write-up attached (FLIGHT-ANALYSIS-2026-11-13.md; section 6 is the part written after the fixes). What matters for the sim:

a. The map frame was tilted. The mapper levelled cuVSLAM's frame with a fixed 20 deg, but that frame is the camera's attitude when the tracker starts. Against gravity the camera was 25.2 deg (vehicle 1) and 29.7 deg (vehicle 3) below the horizontal at start. The cameras are not mounted at 20 deg either: 22.7 deg on vehicle 1 and 27.1 deg on vehicle 3 (PX4's attitude against cuVSLAM's orientation over the flight), and the vehicles rested 2 to 3 deg nose down.

b. The height reference is the downward rangefinder (EKF2_HGT_REF 2, EKF2_RNG_CTRL 2, barometer off, MPC_ALT_MODE 2), with EKF2_EV_CTRL 11. PX4 holds the height above the ground: dist_bottom was 1.00 m (0.87 to 1.11) for the whole flight. The field is not level: it falls about 1.0 deg ahead of the launch line (both vehicles measure it), with the ground between -1.4 and +0.2 m relative to the take-off spots over the flown area.

c. Why the vehicles turned back 30.6 m (vehicle 1) and 16.7 m (vehicle 3) ahead. The frontier finder only looks in a slab of heights, 0.1 to 2.0 m above the take-off spot in the map frame. With the tilted map the whole slab was below the ground from about 28 m ahead of vehicle 1 and 14 m ahead of vehicle 3. Both turned back about 2.7 m past those points. This is my reading of the geometry and it fits both vehicles; I have not reproduced it. Your vehicles spawn level on flat ground with a 20 deg camera, so none of a, b or c shows in the sim today.

d. PX4's forward position was short by cos(pitch + 20 deg): 0.72 on vehicle 1 and 0.65 on vehicle 3 (vio_bridge from px4_offboard dev 87c25c5). The peer poses sent over LoRa are PX4's, so they carried the same error. Sideways everything agreed.

e. Inside the reach of c the exploration was inefficient. The team's 3D map covered 78 percent of the fence after 2 min 45 s and 86 percent at the end. Median hop to the next goal 2.8 m and 2.2 m; 58 and 88 percent of goals within 2.5 m of a fence edge; 40 and 60 percent on ground that vehicle had mapped more than 20 s earlier. Causes in the code: score = gain / (distance + 1 m), so the nearest frontier wins; a free cell on the box boundary counted as a frontier, so the fence edge was a frontier for ever; the lane-sweep fallback could never start because its obstacle density counted unknown cells.

f. A defect in the frontier finder that your runs have too. A group of frontier cells below min_cluster_size was dropped but its cells stayed marked as taken for good, so a frontier that first appeared a few cells at a time could never become a cluster. On the bench, a node that had run since the map was empty held 2 clusters where a fresh node on the same map found 96. In the flight the finder still held 200 to 1,400 clusters, so it did not stop the exploration there, but it loses small pockets late in a run.

g. Rates today: IR and depth 60 Hz at the sensor with the projector alternating, so cuVSLAM, PX4 and the mapper all get 30 Hz; colour 15 Hz.


3. What is on the vehicles now

All four vehicles run the same build since Nov 16. It passed a four-vehicle bench bring-up three times; it has not flown. It is on GitHub:

  active_exploration   feature/jfr-multi-drone-field   a44e9fc   (Nov 13 flew 37c7d32)
  multi_drone_nvblox   feature/jfr-multi-drone-field   c91e04a   (Nov 13 flew 3647995)
  px4_offboard         dev                             180d551   (Nov 13 flew 87c25c5)

The commits that flew on Nov 13 are the parents of these and are on GitHub now as well, so you do not need patches.

- Level from gravity. odom_correction reads the camera accelerometer while the vehicle stands still at tracker start and levels the map with that. It needs /camera0/imu (sensor_msgs/Imu in RealSense optical axes; set imu_optical_axes false if your IMU is in link axes). It publishes the levelled TF and /visual_slam/tracking/odometry_level, and nothing until the level is known. Without a still IMU window it falls back to the fixed 20 deg after imu_wait_s (10 s). To get the old fixed 20 deg on purpose: no IMU topic and imu_wait_s 0.
- The planner and vio_bridge read /visual_slam/tracking/odometry_level. vio_bridge is rewritten on the levelled pose (include/px4_offboard/vio_math.hpp). It replaces the sign-pair fix 7ac8ad7 on your sim branch; when you merge, take dev. Checked by replaying the Nov 13 odometry through it against PX4's own log: yaw 1.00, sideways 1.00, forward 1/0.72 and 1/0.65 of what flew.
- Frontier finder: the fence edge is not a frontier; the marked-cells defect of 2f is fixed; a map request that is never answered no longer blocks every later update. These three are in the code and cannot be switched off.
- Frontier finder, fused map: argument esdf_overlay_service_name. The vehicle's own map stays the source at 1 Hz, and cells it does not know are taken from the shared mapper, so ground a peer has mapped is not a frontier. I did not make the shared mapper the only source because its answers (the whole box at map resolution, 120 MB) reach the frontier finder in phases of 3 to 6 s each on the vehicles, while the own mapper's arrive within a second. I have not found why. Tell me if you see the same in the sim.
- Frontier finder, height slab: argument follow_terrain. The slab follows the ground under the vehicle (levelled camera height minus PX4's dist_bottom, in 0.25 m steps). It needs /fmu/out/vehicle_local_position with dist_bottom valid, so a rangefinder in the sim.
- The fleet launcher gives fis_stage.launch.py both: esdf_overlay_service_name:=/nvblox_shared_node/get_esdf_and_gradient follow_terrain:=true. Their defaults are none and false, and fleet_bringup.launch.py does not pass them through, so give them to fis_stage.launch.py yourself.
- Lane sweep: the trigger now counts known cells only. The mode itself is off (node parameter coverage_mode_enable, default false, in no launch file) and stays off until after the next flights. Leave it off.

Not changed: goal scoring, and ekf2_odom_tf.
